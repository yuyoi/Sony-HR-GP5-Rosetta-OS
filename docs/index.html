Sony HR-GP5 / HR-MP5 Mod: Rosetta OS + DSP synth

New firmware for the Sony HR-GP5 (HRGP5) and HR-MP5 half-rack guitar processors. One chip swap in the stock socket, no board mod. Free, open source, and tested on a real unit.

What the mod adds

Polyphonic DSP synthOur own program on the TMS57002 effects DSP: 8 smooth voices, or 15 raw ones. Sine, saw and pulse with width, ADSR, a stereo resonant filter, stereo spread.
8-bit and crush modesBITS 16 / 12 / 8 / 6 / 4, RATE crush and GLITCH, on all 15 voices.
MIDI in on all channelsVelocity, sustain pedal, chords per key. Every setting on a MIDI CC, so a DAW can play and tweak it live.
Guitar thruTHRU mixes the guitar input in with the synth.
Hex Girl bootThe Rosetta mascot animates on the 228 x 40 LCD at power-on, then the unit starts as normal.
New menusA ROSETTA page on the front panel. On the HR-MP5 firmware the encoder edits values and the rocker moves between rows and pages.
HR-MP5 firmware on an HR-GP5Same board: the stereo HR-MP5 firmware runs on the HR-GP5.

Every feature above has been confirmed on hardware.

How it works

  1. Read your own firmware EPROM (LE27C1001, DIP32) on a programmer such as a T48. Keep the original.
  2. Run tools/rosetta_build.py. It patches your dump and writes the burn file.
  3. Burn a 27C010 / 27C1001, or an SST39SF040 (the image written 4 times), and fit it in the socket. Put the original back and it's a stock unit again.
